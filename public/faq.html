Employee Portal FAQ

Everything you need to know about Gantec sessions and policies

Monthly feedback is a structured evaluation provided by your manager or trainer based on your individual performance. It is provided by your manager, and at the end of each month, you need to collect it from the respective manager on or before the month-end date.

Evaluation Categories:

💻 Technical Delivery – Quality and efficiency of your work
🧠 Solutioning Depth – Problem-solving and understanding
⏱️ Reliability – Consistency and meeting deadlines
📈 Growth – Learning, improvement, and adaptability
🌟 Presence & Communication – Participation, confidence, and clarity
Importance: This process helps you understand your strengths, identifies areas to improve, and supports your overall career growth.

Weekly Connect is a fun and interactive session conducted once a week where everyone comes together for casual conversations, activities, and games.

Certifications are learning programs or courses assigned to improve your skills. They are essential to enhance your knowledge and boost your career opportunities.
