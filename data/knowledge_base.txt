--- LEAVE POLICIES ---
- PTO/Vacation: 16 days per year (4 days per quarter).
- Sick Days: 6 days per year (1.5 days per quarter).
- Celebration Holiday: 1 day for birthday or wedding anniversary (self/spouse/child).
- Leave Without Pay: Up to 365 days for medical, education, or childcare.
- Paternity Leave: 
  * Less than 1 year tenure: 5 working days.
  * 1-2 years tenure: 10 working days.
  * 2+ years tenure: 15 working days.
  * Must be used within 12 months of birth/adoption.

--- CELEBRATION REIMBURSEMENT ---
- Amount: Max INR 5,000 per financial year.
- Usage: Birthday OR Wedding Anniversary (not both).
- Eligibility: Regular full-time employees after probation.
- Eligible Expenses: Food/dining, cakes, decorations, event venue, party supplies.
- Non-Eligible: Alcohol, gifts, jewelry, travel/hotel.
- Process: Submit itemized bills within 15 days of expense. Expense must be within 30 days of the actual event date.
